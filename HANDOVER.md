# Handover

Keep this brief (approximately 300 words or fewer). AI use is expected.

## Priorities
What did you improve first, and why?

## Feature decision
Which scenario did you choose? What was the key design choice, and which reasonable alternative did you reject?

## Deliberately unfinished
What did you leave out, and why?

## Verification
Which commands or checks did you actually run, and what happened? What remains unverified?
